What the numbers say

Three patterns show up in most Metro Manila lead funnels.

Speed beats price

A reply inside five minutes converts about ten times better than a reply the next day. Fast, helpful answers matter more than the lowest price.

Social drives volume

Facebook and Instagram bring the most inquiries, but portal and referral leads often qualify faster. Track quality per channel, not only count.

The viewing is the leak

Only one in four viewings becomes a reservation. Pre viewing questions on budget, financing and timeline lift that rate before anyone visits the unit.
